VocalCoach

It phones you each morning and stays on the line until you know your words

We studied seven vocabulary apps and the learning research behind them

Daily habit keeps people learning

84% of Duolingo's 58.7 million daily learners come back the next day

Spacing helps vocabulary more than grammar or pronunciation

Kim and Webb found this across 98 results from 3,411 learners in 2022

Saying a word out loud teaches you to use it

Nakata showed this in 2015 and Yanagisawa confirmed it in 2016

Anki works best for memory but makes learners grade their own answers

Voice tutors stick to a script

Speak and Praktika drill each lesson's phrases instead of your own words

Each app covers one piece: Duolingo the daily habit, Anki the spaced reviews, voice tutors the speaking

VocalCoach puts all three into one morning call about your own words
